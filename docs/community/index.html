📲 how to install on your phone

  1. Tap download pack on any card above in your flip phone browser.
  2. Open the downloaded .mochibundle (or legacy .mochibun) or .mochibunled file from your notifications or file manager.
  3. Mochibun pops open a live swatch, font, sound & asset preview — hit apply and you're all set ♡ (Rooted users can also sync the pack's font system-wide via Magisk in Settings › Appearance.)

🎀 share your own creation

Export your setup from Settings › Backup & Restore › Export Theme Pack, or open the Mascot & Bundle Workshop 🐰 to create or edit full .mochibundle theme packs right in your browser—bundle a palette, greeting, custom font (font.ttf / font.otf, ≤ 4 MB), keypad sound pack (sounds/<cue>.ogg, ≤ 256 KB per cue), wallpaper, cursor, 9-slice card border, and an 8-row mascot_atlas.png + speech-bubble dialogue!

Drop your file + a screenshot into our GitHub Pack Submission form to add it here ✨
