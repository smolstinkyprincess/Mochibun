🌷 standard setup (no root, the default)

mochibun works right out of the box on a stock flip phone. root-only settings stay hidden so things stay tidy. open settings › permissions & root and walk the little checklist:

  1. default home app: pick mochibun as your home.
  2. display over other apps: lets the cursor float above everything.
  3. mochibun cursor & keys (optional accessibility helper): cursor clicks & scrolls in touch-only apps, plus keypad shortcuts for recents & quick settings.
  4. notification access (optional): powers now playing and the notifications page.

screen-off flip reset tidies everything back to home whenever the screen goes dark, so closing the flip feels clean even without root ♡

🔮 optional root & lsposed perks

rooted with magisk + lsposed? turn on show root & lsposed options at the bottom of settings › permissions & root to wake up key remapping in every app, media volume lock, in-place wi-fi/bluetooth toggles, outer lid leds, and pastel systemui.

✨ new in v2.2.0: lid led studio! draw your own 5-window light shows for the outer lid, step by step with 7 keitai swatches. set one for notifications and one for charging, test them live, and swap them with friends as .mochibunled files ♡
