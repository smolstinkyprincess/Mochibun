Mochibun is a lightweight pastel launcher made for T9 keypad and D-pad Android flip phones, lovingly tuned for the ALT Mive Style Folder (AT-M120, Android 12). It also installs on Android 8.1+ devices with 32-bit/dual-ABI ARM (armeabi-v7a), so other keypad phones can try it too (community-untested).

Features:

- Physical Keypad Support & Interactive Key Guide: Move through apps, sticker-tray folders perched atop the clouds, and Settings Hub cards wearing 1-bit Docomo pixel icons using the physical arrow keys and number pad. Open the interactive AT-M120 Key Guide in Settings to see live key highlights across Launcher, Typing, and Remap tabs.
- T9 App Search: Type numbers on your keypad to quickly filter and find apps without tapping the screen.
- Mochibun Keitai Keyboard: A built-in multi-tap T9 keyboard that works in every app, no extra keyboard app needed, with an adjustable T9 auto-commit delay. Hold # anywhere to pop open the Kaomoji Wheel.
- Virtual Mouse Cursor: Turn on a mouse pointer with the side key (or a Menu, Search, Camera or * key of your choice) so you can click and scroll in apps made only for touchscreens. Works without root through an optional Accessibility helper.
- Keypad Sounds & Soft Keys: 7 built-in keypad sound profiles (including Muted) plus custom .ogg sound packs from .mochibundle themes, LSK / RSK soft-key navigation you can toggle, and hold 0 on Home for the flashlight.
- Mochibunny Pocket Pet: Press Center on the bunny on Home (or tap her) for a Feed / Pet / Chat pill, with 1 / 2 / 3 keypad shortcuts. Treats come from steps, clearing notifications, and plugging in your charger, and her mood and speech bubbles change through the day.
- Theme Studio & All-in-One .mochibundle Packs: 5 built-in palettes (Sakura Dream, Lavender Mist, Minty Matcha, Milky Peach, and the Midnight Mochi dark theme), custom YAML themes, and shareable .mochibundle packs with bundled fonts (font.ttf / font.otf, up to 4 MB), keypad sound packs (sounds/<cue>.ogg, up to 256 KB per cue), greeting, wallpaper, custom cursor, 9-slice card border, and an 8-row mascot_atlas.png sprite sheet + dialogue (plus optional Magisk system-wide font sync for rooted users).
- Pastel SystemUI: With the optional LSPosed module, your palette also themes Quick Settings, the volume dialog, notification cards, and the status and navigation bars.
- Media Volume Lock: The volume rocker always steps media volume, even in Silent Mode.
- Widgets, Recents & Music: A widget page for your third-party widgets with a two-band Now Playing card, plus a keypad-friendly recents switcher.
- Clamshell Lid Actions: Choose what happens when you open the lid. Flip Reset on Sleep brings you back Home when the screen turns off, and with root you get notification pulses on the outer lid LEDs.
- Lid LED Studio (root): Draw your own 5-window light shows for the outer lid, step by step with 7 vivid keitai swatches. Set one for notifications and one for charging, test it live on the lid, and share it as a .mochibunled file.
- Offline & Private: No internet or camera permissions, no telemetry, and canRetrieveWindowContent="false" on the optional Accessibility service (it never reads screen text).
- Lightweight & Fast: No heavy GPU blurs, tuned for 480 x 800 screens and ~1.8 GB of RAM.

No root needed: the launcher, keyboard, Kaomoji Wheel, themes, and virtual cursor all work on a stock phone. A simple setup checklist in Settings > Permissions & Root walks you through it: Default Home App, Display Over Other Apps, the optional "Mochibun Cursor & Keys" Accessibility helper (cursor clicks/scrolls plus Recents and Quick Settings shortcuts), and Notification Access. Flip Reset on Sleep sends you back Home whenever the screen turns off. Root-only settings stay hidden until you turn on "Show Root Options". With Magisk + the built-in LSPosed module you also get visual key remapping in every app via the interactive Keypad Remapper, Media Volume Lock, in-place Wi-Fi/Bluetooth toggles, optional system-wide font sync, outer lid LEDs with the Lid LED Studio, and SystemUI theming.
